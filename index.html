THE MOVEMENT

WHERE PASSION MEETS STREET ENERGY

Founded in Tunisia (+216), our studio was created to give dancers, hip-hop artists, and choreographers a high-octane sanctuary to train, collaborate, and take their skills to national and international stages.

From hosting masterclasses with top choreographers to directing backstage tour performances for leading North African recording artists, +216 Dance Studio represents the heart of modern street culture.
